Before you reserve

Common questions

Is the price all-in?

Yes. The list price already includes 12% VAT, registration and miscellaneous fees such as documentary stamp tax, transfer tax and other fees for the transfer of the title.

Can I build my own house design?

Yes. Crown Asia describes Siena's lots as giving buyers the freedom to design a Tuscan-inspired home. Ask me about the developer's building guidelines before you finalize your plans.

How much is the reservation fee?

₱100,000 for a lot, based on the official sample computations. The reservation fee is non-refundable and non-transferable, so reserve once you've chosen your lot.

What do I need after reserving?

Fill out the Customer Information Sheet and sign the Option to Purchase Agreement. Complete documents are due within 30 days from reservation. I'll guide you through each one, even if you're abroad.

What if the bank doesn't approve my loan?

According to the official terms, in-house financing automatically applies if bank financing is not approved for any reason. Ask me for the in-house terms.

Who do I pay?

All payments go directly to Household Development Corp., the developer entity on the official documents. Downpayments and amortizations are covered by post-dated checks.

When is the turnover?

The target turnover is Q4 2028, per the developer's official fact sheet. Siena is a pre-selling development, so ask me how your payment schedule lines up with the turnover.
